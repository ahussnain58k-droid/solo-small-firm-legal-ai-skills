# Solo & Small-Firm Legal AI Skills

Practical legal AI workflows, templates, tools, and examples for solo lawyers,
small firms, boutique practices, fractional general counsel, and new attorneys.

No coding is required to begin.

## Start here

1. Read `START-HERE.md`.
2. Review `LEGAL-AI-SAFETY.md`.
3. Open `skills/` and choose your legal task.
4. Review the related template or example.
5. Verify every output before using it for a client or matter.

## Current skills

- Contract template synthesis
- Employment-law research
- Privilege and AI-safety checking
- Redline email drafting
- Startup formation support
- Texas title analysis
- Italian case-law research

## Important

Do not upload confidential or privileged client information into an AI service
unless your firm has approved that service and its security arrangements.

This repository is educational and experimental. It is not legal advice.
