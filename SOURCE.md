# Source

This repository includes, preserves, reorganizes, or adapts material from the HeyCounsel Community legal AI skills registry.

Original project:

`bstevescherer/heycounsel-community`

Original source material is preserved in:

`source-original/heycounsel-community/`

Public beginner-facing material is organized into:

- `skills/`
- `agents/`
- `playbooks/`
- `templates/`
- `tools/`
- `examples/`

The repository structure was changed to make the material easier for solo lawyers, small firms, and non-technical legal users to understand.
